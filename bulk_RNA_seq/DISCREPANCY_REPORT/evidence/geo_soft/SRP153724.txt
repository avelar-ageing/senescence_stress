!Sample_treatment_protocol_ch1 = The proliferating and contact-inhibited cells were treated with actinomycin D at a concentration of 15 μg/ml. Cells were harvested at different time points (2, 4, 6, and 8 hours) after actinomycin treatment using Trizol.
!Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = Proliferating fibroblasts (5 x 10^5 cells) were grown in a 10 cm plate uisng a complete medium containing DMEM and 10 % fetal bovine serum. Proliferating cells were grown to a confluency of 60-70% before treatment. For contact inhibition, the cells were plated under the same conditions as proliferating cells and allowed to grow for 7 days after plating to make the cells contact-inhibited.
!Sample_molecule_ch1 = total RNA
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = Actinomycin D-treated cells were lysed using 1 ml of Trizol (Life Technologies) per 10 cm plate for 5 min at room temperature. RNA was isolated from the lysed cells using the Direct-zol™ RNA MiniPrep Plus kit (Zymo Research) by following the manufacturer's instructions.The concentration of RNA was measured using a Nanodrop 2000c (Thermo Fisher Scientific).
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = cDNA libraries enriched in 3' end fragrments were generated with Nextera kit using Smart-seq2 cDNA amplification method (Picelli et al., PMID: 24385147). Common forward primers were used for all samples; reverse primers with a unique barcode sequence (i5 indices) were specific for each sample. The size distribution of the cDNA library was confirmed on a Bioanalyzer 2100 Instrument (Agilent Technologies). Libraries (150-1000 bp) were subjected to gel size selection to enrich for fragments in the 180 – 280 size range. The concentration of the final library was measured on a qubit fluorometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific).
!Sample_data_processing = The reads were trimmed to remove the polyA tail and adapter sequences. Reads were aligned to human genome (hg19) using Tophat (v2.0.14,standard parameters) to generate bam files.
!Sample_data_processing = To obtain the counts for polyadenylation sites at each time point, the bam files were used as input to perl code provided by Gruber et al. (PMID: 27382025): http://polyasite.unibas.ch/. PolyA site atlas by Gruber et al. was used as the annotation for polyadenylation sites.
!Sample_data_processing = Only the polyadenylation sites present in terminal exons (TEs) were considered.
