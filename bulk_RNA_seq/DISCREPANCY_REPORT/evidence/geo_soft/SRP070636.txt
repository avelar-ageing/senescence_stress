!Sample_treatment_protocol_ch1 = Senescence was induced with 4-OHT (Sigma-Aldrich). Senescent cells were maintained in dishes for 10 d to ensure growth termination. Senescence was determined by monitoring CDKN2A/P16 up-regulation and down-regulation of cyclin genes and by SA-β-gal (Chemicon International).
!Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = IMR90 cells (obtained from Coriell Institute for Medical Research) were grown in DMEM without phenol with 10% FBS and 1% penicillin/streptomycin at 3% oxygen. The cells were generated by retrovirally infecting normal IMR90 fibroblasts with pLNCX-ER:Ras. For the viral transfections, IMR90 fibroblasts were infected with virus (either scrambled control (SC) or MLL1 KD shRNA (MLL1 KD)  at 60% confluency on 10-cm2 plates for 24 h in the presence of polybrene. Forty-eight hours following infections, cells underwent selection with puromycin to obtain completely puromycin-resistant cell populations.
!Sample_molecule_ch1 = polyA RNA
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = RNA was extracted from whole cells using RNeasy kit (Qiagen catalog no. 74014) following the manufacturer's instructions.
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = Following Poly-A selection, the RNA-seq libraries for the WT (SC), WT OIS (SC OIS), and MLL1 knockdown (Control and OIS) cells were made using a ScriptSeq version 2 RNA-seq library preparation kit from Epicentre (now Illumina).
!Sample_data_processing = Alignment: data were aligned using RNA STAR version 2.3.0e under default settings, Homo sapiens ENSEMBL release 75.
!Sample_data_processing = Expression quantitation: Cufflinks version 2.2.1 was used to map aligned tags to exons and gene features (-G parameter) and tabulate expression scores. The ENSEMBL Homo sapiens release GRCh37.75 was used as the feature table. All other Cufflinks settings were the default parameters.
!Sample_data_processing = Track creation: aligned tags were binned in 100bp contiguous, non-overlapping bins and were multiplied by a per-10-million aligned tag adjustment coefficient (reads were counted to 100-bp fixed bins using featureCounts from the Subreads package version 1.4.6-p2).
