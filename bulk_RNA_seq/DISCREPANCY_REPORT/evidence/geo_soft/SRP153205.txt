!Sample_treatment_protocol_ch1 = Proliferating fibroblasts were treated with siRNA (10 cm culture dish) using Genmute transfection reagent (Signagen® Laboratories) following the manufactuter's instructions. Cell were harvested 48 hours after transfection for RNA extraction.
!Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = Fibroblasts were grown in complete medium (DMEM with 10% fetal bovine serum).
!Sample_molecule_ch1 = total RNA
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = Harvested cells were lysed using 1 ml of Trizol (Life Technologies) per 10 cm plate for 5 min at room temperature. RNA was isolated from the lysed cells using the Direct-zol™ RNA MiniPrep Plus kit (Zymo Research)  following the manufacturer's instructions. The concentration of RNA was measured using Nanodrop 2000c (Thermo Fisher Scientific).
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = cDNA libraries enriched in fragrments from 3´ ends of transcripts were generated with a Nextera kit using Smart-seq2 cDNA amplification method (Picelli et al., PMID: 24385147). Common forward primers were used for all samples; reverse primers with a unique barcode sequence (i5 indices) were specific for each sample. The size distribution of the cDNA library was confirmed on a Bioanalyzer 2100 Instrument (Agilent Technologies). Libraries (150-1000 bp) were subjected to gel size selection to enrich for 180 – 280 bp sized fragments. The concentration of the final library was measured on a qubit fluorometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific).
!Sample_data_processing = The reads were trimmed to remove polyA tail and adapter sequences. Reads were aligned to human genome (hg19) using Tophat (v2.0.14, standard parameters used) to generate bam files.
!Sample_data_processing = To obtain the counts for polyadenylation sites, the bam files were used as input to perl code provided by Gruber et al. (PMID: 27382025): http://polyasite.unibas.ch/. PolyA site atlas by Gruber et al. was used as the annotation for polyadenylation sites.
!Sample_data_processing = Only the polyadenylation sites present in terminal exon (TE), exon except terminal exon (EX), and intron (IN) were considered.
